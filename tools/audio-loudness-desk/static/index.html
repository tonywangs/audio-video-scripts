Know what changed.

Measured, not judged. LUFS measures integrated loudness, not subjective quality. A louder recording is not necessarily a better one. No human listening evaluation is implied.

Peaks take priority. Gain is capped with 0.3 dB headroom. No compression or limiting. Unreachable targets are flagged and included as incomplete in the export manifest.

Recover by identity. Only settings are saved in this browser and project JSON. Reattach the exact source files after reload; SHA-256 must match before settings are restored.
