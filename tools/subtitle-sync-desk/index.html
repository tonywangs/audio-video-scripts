Real-media demo and known limitations

Sintel © Blender Foundation · CC BY 3.0. The full film includes its credits. Demo variants deliberately add +2 seconds or 1% drift +2 seconds to the official English track. These are labeled perturbations, not defects in the original track.

Linear timing cannot repair arbitrary cuts or nonlinear edits. Overlaps may be intentional. Only plain-text UTF-8 SRT and WebVTT are supported; tags, cue settings and metadata are rejected. Playback codecs depend on your browser. Video stays on disk and must be reselected after reload.
